## What's new in 1.7.0

### 1.7.0 in brief
- **Save plot for publication** — the plot in a journal's column width, on a page or a slide,
  as TIFF / PNG at 300, 600 or 1200 dpi or as PDF / SVG / EPS, with a preview; the legend
  anywhere, texts such as cm⁻¹ or H₂O, printing. Also for the 2D Map and the analysis windows.
  [Help](help://user_guide#publication)
- **Peak Fitting with a background** — a spectrum on an offset or a sloped baseline (e.g. raw
  Raman counts) is fitted without a baseline correction first: the background is fitted
  together with the peaks. [Help](help://peak_fitting#background)
- **Uncertainties in Peak Fitting** — every fitted value with its ± (e.g. position 3432 ± 10),
  also in Copy Results and the Series results; a column *Needed?* says whether each peak is
  needed. [Help](help://peak_fitting#uncertainty)
- **Fix and link values, fit a series together** — keep a value fixed or tie it to another peak
  (the same width, a fixed distance, a fixed height ratio); fit all selected spectra with one
  set of positions and widths, much more stable for noisy series.
  [Help](help://peak_fitting#together)
- **The mouse on the plot** (as in Origin or Excel) — the wheel zooms, the left button held
  moves the plot, pointing at a line shows its spectrum's name, a click on the legend hides a
  spectrum; also in the plots of the tool windows. [Help](help://user_guide#plot-mouse)
- **keep zoom and scale each** — zoomed in on a band, the plot stays there while you go
  through the spectra (also in the tool windows); *scale each* draws every spectrum from 0 to 1
  to compare their shapes. [Help](help://user_guide#keep-zoom)
- **Band markers** — a click on the plot with the Cursor ticked adds one; right-click: the
  spectra's values at the markers, or between two markers (area, the band above its
  background, …) as a table for Excel; Zoom to the band markers. [Help](help://band_markers)
- **Save snapshot and Export spectra** — Ctrl+S saves your work into the snapshot you opened,
  without a dialog; File › Export spectra… writes the spectra for other programs.
  [Help](help://user_guide#save-export)
- **The spectra list** — your own order (drag the spectra), more ways of sorting, one clear
  rule for which spectra are selected after an operation. [Help](help://user_guide#own-order)
- **Find things faster** — search all help pages at once (F1), find a tool by a word (e.g.
  *baseline*, *pca*), a start page with the files opened last.
  [Help](help://user_guide#search-help)
- **"A new version is available"** — the program tells you when a newer version is released,
  with a link to download it. [Help](help://installation#updates)

### New
- **Save plot for publication…** (File menu, right-click on the plot, or the Publication button
  on the top toolbar): the plot in a journal's column width (8.5 / 11.4 / 17.5 cm), a page, a
  slide or your own size, one text size, lines at least so thick, as TIFF / PNG at 300, 600 or
  1200 dpi or as PDF / SVG / EPS. The plot on the screen stays as it is.
  [Help](help://user_guide#publication)
  - A preview in which the legend, titles and labels can be dragged; the legend at a side or
    corner, inside or outside the plot.
  - Tabs like Origin's (only those that apply): font, a size for each kind of text (axis
    titles, numbers, legend, title, labels), axis titles, ranges, ticks (the numbers of an axis
    can be hidden), frame, grid, colour sets (e.g. colour-blind safe, black and white), each
    spectrum's colour, line and marker, a heatmap's colours.
  - Texts with superscript, subscript, Greek letters and signs (e.g. cm⁻¹, H₂O, λ) in a small
    editor; undo / redo; settings saved as profiles.
  - It starts with a whole A4 page (A4 landscape, 26.7 cm wide × 18 cm high, 12 pt text); the
    sizes have clear names ("A4 portrait, half page", …); *Real size* in the preview is
    measured from the screen's real size.
  - Printing with a print preview: fit to page, upright or sideways, grey, a caption; on A4
    paper unless another paper is chosen (Printing › Paper).
  - **2D Map**: right-click on the map or on its spectrum (or the new button in their toolbars):
    the map with its colour bar, or the spectrum. The + of the clicked pixel, the outlines of
    the regions (ROI), the numbered reference pixels and the shaded range can each be left out.
    [Help](help://map2d#publication)
  - **The analysis windows** - Peak Fitting, the Peak list plot, SVD, PCA, MCR-ALS, NMF,
    Cluster analysis, SOM, QC / Outlier, PLS, Melting curve, Kinetics, Band ratio, Isosbestic
    points, 2D correlation, Reference matching, Mixture analysis: a Publication button in each
    plot's toolbar; Group Statistics: Publication… next to Save plot….
- **Peak Fitting**:
  - **Background** (above the Amplitude Constraint): *Automatic* (default), *None*, *Constant*
    or *Linear*. Automatic chooses for each spectrum and shows its choice, e.g. "Automatic
    (Linear)": *None* when the data come back to zero (CD spectra with bands up and down,
    baseline-corrected spectra), *Linear* when they sit on an offset (raw Raman counts). The
    peaks' heights and areas are above the background. Before the fit its first guess is drawn
    (black dash-dot line) and the starting peaks sit on it. The total fit and the residual
    (also the spectra added by OK) include it. In a series each spectrum gets its own
    background. [Help](help://peak_fitting#background)
  - **− background** next to Data above the plot: the data and the fit drawn without the
    background, the peaks from zero (as after a baseline correction). Only the drawing
    changes. **Data: Points / Line**: the measured data as points (default) or as a line.
    [Help](help://peak_fitting#viewing)
  - **Uncertainties (±)** of every fitted value: e.g. Center 3432 ± 10, Height 11.41 ± 0.22
    (the value rounded to its uncertainty); FWHM and area by error propagation; *n/a* where the
    data cannot determine a value (e.g. two peaks at the same place). **± shown as**: 1 σ
    (68 %, default), 2 σ (95 %) or 3 σ (99.7 %). Copy Results puts each ± in the next column,
    the Series results have a ± column after each value.
    [Help](help://peak_fitting#uncertainty)
  - **Is a peak needed?** A column *Needed?*: after Fit Peaks each peak is left out once and
    the spectrum fitted again; the two fits are compared (ΔBIC): *yes*, *probably* or *no*
    (e.g. of two peaks on one band one is enough). Also in Copy Results and the Series results.
    [Help](help://peak_fitting#needed)
  - **Fixed and linked values**: right-click a value in the table of peaks - *Fixed* (not
    fitted; grey, [fixed]), *Keep the distance to peak…* (e.g. [P1+12]), *Keep the height ratio
    to peak…* ([P1×0.5]), *The same width as peak…* ([=P1]), *The same width for all … peaks*,
    *Fitted (free)*; for several selected rows at once. [Help](help://peak_fitting#fix-link)
  - **Several spectra: Together** (box Fitting): all selected spectra fitted at once - *the
    same positions and widths* (only the heights and the background each spectrum's own) or
    *the same widths*. Example: 7 noisy Raman spectra, a small band - one by one its position
    from 413 ± 6 to 438 ± 6; together 428.1 ± 2.2 for all. [Help](help://peak_fitting#together)
  - **Several spectra shown**: ◀ ▶ (Alt+Left / Alt+Right) choose the shown one among the
    selected spectra, which all stay shown; it is framed in orange in the list and marked in
    the plot. Add via Click puts the peak on the spectrum you click in (Overlay: the shown one).
  - **The width of a clicked peak**: *width from the band* (default; measured on smoothed data,
    so noise does not make it narrow) or *fixed width σ*.
    [Help](help://peak_fitting#adding-peaks)
  - **Sorting the peaks**: a click on the heading Center, Height or Width sorts the peaks of
    all spectra; a peak added is put in its place by position.
    [Help](help://peak_fitting#sorting)
  - **Series results**: peak numbers matched by position (a band split into two in some spectra
    still gets the same number); **Hide:** the spectra not fitted yet and values you do not need;
    **Transposed** also for By peak; values you set by hand after a fit are kept, marked ✎;
    select rows and press Delete to remove peaks. [Help](help://peak_fitting#series-results)
- **Band markers and pinned cursors are one list**: a click on the plot with the Cursor ticked
  (or right-click › Add a band marker here) adds a band marker, labelled with its x above the
  plot; it is in the Band markers window with the others (name, colour, style) and saved in
  snapshots. [Help](help://band_markers)
  - Right-click › **Copy the values at the band markers**: the spectra's values at every marker
    for Excel (columns headed by the markers' names).
  - Right-click between two markers › **Copy the values between the band markers 1480 and 1500
    (Δ = 20)**: for every spectrum its maximum, minimum, area, and the band measured from the
    straight line joining its ends (position, height, area, centroid).
    [Help](help://user_guide#between-markers)
  - Right-click between two markers › **Zoom to the band markers 1480 – 1500**: the plot shows
    exactly that band (y fitted). [Help](help://user_guide#zoom-to-markers)
- **keep zoom** (Basic plot options, beside Cursor; on): zoomed in on a band, the plot stays
  there when you select other spectra or step through them with ↑ / ↓ - y is fitted to the
  spectra shown; Home shows the whole range; an orange note beside the plot's toolbar says
  "Zoomed: 1450–1520 – Home shows all". A snapshot opens at the range it was saved with. Also in
  the tool windows that show one spectrum after another (Peak Fitting, the baseline windows,
  Smoothing, Normalization, Spike removal, Interactive subtraction). Untick it for the old
  behaviour. [Help](help://user_guide#keep-zoom)
- **scale each** (Basic plot options, beside reverse x; also right-click on the plot): every
  spectrum drawn from 0 to 1, so spectra of very different intensity are compared by their
  shape; zoomed in on a band, each spectrum is fitted to that band. Or right-click on a band
  marker › Scale each spectrum to 1 at the band marker (e.g. an internal standard, Phe at
  1004). Only the drawing changes - the Cursor and the copied values are the real ones.
  Overlay, Waterfall and Grid plot. [Help](help://user_guide#scale-each)
- **Copy the data in view** (right-click on the plot): the spectra shown, only within the x
  range in view (zoomed on a band: that band), as a table for Excel.
  [Help](help://user_guide#plot-export)
- **Labels on the plot** (right-click on the plot): label a band (its position, e.g. 1004,
  above the peak), add text or an arrow; drag them with the mouse; a list to change or delete
  them; saved in snapshots and in Save plot for publication.
  [Help](help://user_guide#plot-labels)
- **Save snapshot and Export spectra, as in other programs**: **File › Save snapshot** (Ctrl+S)
  saves into the snapshot you opened or saved last, without a dialog (the first time it asks
  for a name), **Save snapshot as…** (Ctrl+Shift+S) into a new one. **File › Export spectra…**
  (Ctrl+E; the former Save…): text, Excel, GRAMS (.spc). The window's title shows the
  snapshot's name, with a * while there are unsaved changes; closing then asks Save / Discard /
  Cancel. [Help](help://user_guide#save-export)
- **Your own order of the spectra**: select spectra and drag the handle (six dots at the left of
  a row) to their place (or Alt+Up / Alt+Down, or right-click › Order); the order counts in the
  plot, the tool windows and exports and is saved in snapshots.
  [Help](help://user_guide#own-order)
- **Sorting the list** (Sort: ▾ above the list): by name, in the order added, by a property
  (temperature, pH, …), by a number in the name, or your own order; reverse for each.
  [Help](help://user_guide#sorting)
- **Search the help** (Help menu, F1): a word in all help pages at once; a double-click opens
  the page there. [Help](help://user_guide#search-help)
- **Search a tool**: a field *Search…* next to the drop-down lists of Spectra Processing and
  Spectra Analysis & Visualization: type a part of a tool's name or a word for what it does
  (e.g. *baseline*, *smooth*, *snv*, *pca*, *melting*); a small typo is forgiven.
  [Help](help://user_guide#pipeline)
- **Start page**: while no spectra are loaded, the plot area shows big buttons - Import data,
  Open snapshot, Try a test dataset, Quick start - and the files opened last.
  [Help](help://user_guide#start-page)
- **A new version**: a few seconds after the start the program asks GitHub whether a newer
  version is released and if so says so: *Open the download page*, *Skip this version*,
  *Later*. **Help › Check for a new version…** asks at any time. One short request to GitHub;
  nothing about you or your data is sent; without internet nothing happens. Switch it off in
  File › Settings. [Help](help://installation#updates)

### Easier to use
- **The mouse on the plot**: the mouse wheel zooms where the mouse is (x; with Ctrl y; Home goes
  back); the left button held moves the plot, without choosing Pan first; pointing at a line
  shows its spectrum's name and a click on it finds it in the list; a click on a name in the
  legend hides that spectrum, a second click shows it again (Mean ± SD, Difference and Heatmap
  are then computed without it; processing still uses all selected spectra).
  [Help](help://user_guide#plot-mouse)
- **The same in the tool windows**: in the plots of Peak Fitting, SVD, PCA, NMF, 2D Map,
  Kinetics, Open in New Window and the others with a plot toolbar the wheel zooms and the left
  button held moves the plot - except where a click or a drag already does something (adds a
  point, draws a range).
- **The mouse over a name in the spectra list** draws its line thicker and on top - which line
  is sp007 among eleven.
- **Zoom or Pan on is easy to see**: the pressed-in button has an orange frame and an orange
  note beside the buttons says how to end it. **Esc** switches off Zoom or Pan (in a tool window
  without closing it); in the main window, pressed again, the Cursor. Esc closes every tool
  window, also after a click on its plot.
- **Zoomed in, nothing floats beside the plot**: peak positions, band markers' numbers and your
  labels whose point is out of view are not drawn outside the plot, and the plot no longer gets
  narrower to make room for them. Numbers close together are written one above the other.
- **Which spectra are selected afterwards - one clear rule**: Apply keeps the selection; new
  spectra (Add as New, copies, an added import, results of tools) are selected; Undo and the
  History bring back the selection of that step; Alt+Left / Alt+Right go back and forward like a
  browser. [Help](help://user_guide#selection-after)
- **Plot type "Single spectrum" removed**: it was an Overlay of one spectrum. Click one in the
  list and press ↓ / ↑ to go through the spectra one by one. An older snapshot with "Single
  spectrum" opens as an Overlay plot.
- **Spectrum properties like a spreadsheet**: Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V (a block from Excel),
  one value pasted into many cells, Ctrl+D fills down, Delete, Ctrl+Z; also in a right-click
  menu. [Help](help://user_guide#spectrum-properties)
- **The window opens as you left it**: its size, place (also maximized, on a second monitor) and
  the width of the spectra list.
- **A legend dragged to another place stays there** when *show legend* is ticked again or the
  Legend window's settings are applied (unless its Position is changed).
- **Clearer tooltips and "?" in every window**: the names of the options are bold, one choice
  per line, and each orange "?" sits right after what it explains.
- **Automated Baseline**: its list follows the ↑ / ↓ keys as the other windows' lists do (the
  preview is computed once the keys stop).
- **Interactive subtraction** opens beside the plot like the other processing windows: zoom and
  the Basic plot options stay usable.
- **Peak Fitting** opens again with the spectra selected last time and as shown last time
  (Overlay, Waterfall or Grid); the columns of the table of peaks can be made wider and the
  column Extra is shown only for Voigt and Pseudo-Voigt peaks; the left part scrolls, so the
  line between it and the plot moves freely.

### Fixed
- **Tables sorted by a column** (Spectrum properties, Rename spectra, Melting Curve Analysis):
  Delete, Paste, Fill down, Add prefix and similar actions wrote some values into the wrong rows
  or skipped cells, because the rows moved while they were written.
- **Copy cursor values in a Waterfall plot** (now Copy the values at the band markers) copied the
  values as drawn (shifted up); now the spectra's own values.
- **Manual baseline and Spike removal** could not be closed with the X of their window.
- **The counter above the spectra list** (e.g. 5/36) kept the old numbers after Add as New or a
  tool's results until you clicked in the list.
- **Peak Fitting of noisy data on an offset** did not converge: the peaks had to make the whole
  offset. Now the background takes it.
- **The y axis of the Peak Fitting plot** was stretched far beyond the data after Auto-Detect
  Peaks or Add via Click on data on an offset. Now it shows the range of the data and the fit.
- **Peak Fitting marked good fits of noisy data ⚠** (their R² is low because noise cannot be
  fitted). Now ⚠ also needs a residual clearly larger than the noise.
- **The table of peaks** took any text (e.g. letters typed into Center); the peak then kept its
  old value without showing it. Now only numbers can be typed.
- **A clicked peak on noisy data** got a very narrow width (a point of noise); a band split into
  two maxima by noise is now found once.
- **A peak added after removing one** could get the colour of another peak.
- **The mouse wheel in Peak Fitting** changed a drop-down list (e.g. the Model of a peak) when
  the mouse was over it. Now it only scrolls.
- **Peak Fitting closed before Fit Peaks and opened again** drew the starting peaks as if they
  were a fit.
- **OK with Output Options for Voigt and Pseudo-Voigt peaks**: the added spectra (total fit,
  residual, single peaks) were calculated wrongly or OK stopped with an error. Now they match the
  plot.
